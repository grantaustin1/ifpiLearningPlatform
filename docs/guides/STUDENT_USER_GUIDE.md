# IFPI Learning Platform — Student User Guide

**Version 1.0 · July 2026**

Welcome! This short guide walks you through everything you can do as a
learner: finding and taking courses, live sessions, exams, certificates,
and keeping your learning streak alive.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Creating your account
- If you received an **invitation email**, click the link and set your
  password — you'll land directly in your academy.
- Otherwise open the platform URL and choose **Register**. After signing up,
  check your inbox for a verification email and click the link (you can
  resend it from the banner in the app).

### 1.2 Logging in
Sign in with your email and password. If your academy has enabled two-factor
authentication you'll also enter a 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

### 1.3 Your dashboard
Your home screen shows:
- courses you're enrolled in, with progress bars
- your **learning streak** (consecutive active days) and points
- badges you've earned and the leaderboard
- upcoming live sessions you've RSVP'd to

---

## 2. Finding & Enrolling in Courses

### 2.1 The catalog
Browse the **Catalog** for your academy's published courses. Each card shows
the description, estimated duration and price (if any). The **Marketplace**
may also show courses from partner academies.

### 2.2 Enrolling
- **Free courses** — click Enrol and start immediately.
- **Paid courses** — click Enrol and complete the secure checkout. Your
  access activates as soon as payment is confirmed. Your receipts and
  subscription status are available under **Billing**.

### 2.3 Learning paths
If your academy uses **Learning Paths**, courses are arranged in a sequence —
finish one step to unlock the next. Your position on the path is always
visible.

---

## 3. Taking a Course

### 3.1 The course player
Courses are made of slides. Use the arrows (or arrow keys) to move through
them; your progress is saved automatically, so you can leave and pick up
exactly where you stopped. Some slides include audio narration — press play
to listen.

### 3.2 The AI Tutor
Stuck on a concept? Click **Ask AI Tutor** inside any course. The tutor
answers using your academy's course materials and shows citations for where
the answer came from. Your conversation history is saved per session; only
you can see it.

> Privacy note: any personal information in your questions is automatically
> redacted before the question is processed by the AI.

### 3.3 Flashcards
If your course has flashcards, you'll find them under **Flashcards**. The
review scheduler shows you each card just before you're about to forget it
(spaced repetition). You can also save useful AI Tutor answers as flashcards
with one click.

---

## 4. Live Sessions

- **RSVP** to upcoming sessions from the course page or the sessions list.
  If the session belongs to a course you're not enrolled in yet, you'll be
  enrolled automatically when you RSVP.
- **Recurring series** — RSVP once for the whole series or per occurrence.
- **Calendar** — subscribe to your personal calendar feed (ICS) so sessions
  appear in Google/Apple/Outlook calendars, or download individual invites.
- **Reminders** — you'll get an email reminder before each session you've
  RSVP'd to.
- **Attendance** — your instructor marks attendance; attending can earn you
  an attendance certificate automatically.

---

## 5. Exams & Certificates

### 5.1 Taking an exam
When you finish the slides, take the course assessment. Note:
- exams may have a **time limit** — the timer is visible throughout
- you have a limited number of **attempts** (shown before you start)
- you need the **passing score** (e.g. 70%) to earn your certificate

Your results appear immediately after submission.

### 5.2 Your certificates
Pass an exam (or attend a qualifying session) and your certificate is issued
automatically, branded by your academy. From **Certificates** you can:

- **Download the PDF** — includes a QR code for instant verification
- **Share** — every certificate has a public share page that renders a rich
  preview on LinkedIn and Twitter/X
- **Verify** — anyone with the link (or QR scan) can confirm your
  certificate is genuine, without needing an account

---

## 6. Streaks, Points & Badges

- **Streak** — any meaningful activity (slides, exams, flashcards) keeps
  your daily streak alive. We'll email you a nudge if your streak is about
  to break.
- **Points** — earned for completing slides, exams and sessions.
- **Badges** — awarded automatically as you cross point thresholds set by
  your academy.
- **Leaderboard** — see how you rank against other learners in your academy.

---

## 7. Your Account

- **Profile & password** — change your password anytime under Settings. If
  you forget it, use "Forgot password" on the login page.
- **Two-factor authentication** — optionally protect your account with an
  authenticator app; recovery codes are provided at setup.
- **Notifications** — opt in/out of digest and reminder emails under
  **Preferences**.
- **Terms & Conditions** — if your academy publishes new terms you'll be
  asked to accept them before continuing.
- **Your data (GDPR)** — you can request an export of your data, or account
  erasure. Erasure anonymises your personal details while keeping issued
  certificates verifiable.

---

## 8. FAQ

**My progress didn't save?**
Progress saves automatically per slide. Check your connection and reopen the
course — you'll resume at the last slide you completed.

**I failed the exam. Can I retry?**
Yes, up to the attempt limit shown on the exam start screen. Review the
slides and flashcards before retrying.

**My certificate link shows "revoked".**
Certificates can be revoked by your academy (e.g. issued in error). Contact
your academy administrator.

**I didn't get the verification / invitation email.**
Check spam, then use the resend button. If it still doesn't arrive, ask your
administrator — they can copy your invite link directly from their console.

**Who can see my AI Tutor questions?**
Only you. Sessions are private to your account, and personal information is
redacted automatically before AI processing.
